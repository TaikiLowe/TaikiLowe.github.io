SamaCare

Redesigning an internal operations tool to help healthcare teams work with more clarity.

Role
Product designer
Team
Client engagement team
Focus
Operations UX

Introduction

SamaCare is a healthcare technology company seeking to automate Prior Authorization (PA) workflows. Their platform streamlines the submission, tracking, and management of these PA requests across payers and drugs, helping practices cut authorization time in half while ensuring compliance with payer-specific rules. By automating repetitive tasks, integrating seamlessly with any EMR system, and centralizing communication, SamaCare empowers providers to spend less time on paperwork and more time on patient care. Their mission is to remove barriers to treatment access by simplifying one of healthcare’s most time-consuming administrative processes.

Stage 1: User Research

We interviewed Marissa Jaros and Jason Bernzwig, who work at SamaCare and use their tool on a day-to-day basis to manage forms. The goal of this interview was to gain insights on any pain points and inefficiencies involved in the overall workflow of using the tool, specifically with its main form management tab, Ops Hubs. Our overall findings were that the tool was misaligned with the tasks it was being used for, leading its users to depend on external tools, and that experiencing these difficulties at each step increases cognitive load and frustration with the tool. From there, we found three main pain points:

• Importing PDFs and forms had to be done using a separate tab from Ops Hub, meaning that teams had to switch between the two tabs to manage forms and updates.

• Because the internal Ops Hub did not contain critical features including version tracking and update history, users of the tool depend on using Monday Board, which is an external tool that has these features. This would result in all updates needing to be entered twice — once on Ops Hub and once on Monday Board.

• Authorization forms and fax numbers were being managed in separate tabs, meaning that teams had to switch between tabs to see the full context of a form and edit its associated fax numbers.

Taking these pain points into consideration, we were able to begin ideation on a full design overhaul of the tool.

Stage 2: Ideation & Planning

Based on our user interview results, we constructed a new workflow that would address the tool's pain points. We decided to integrate all essential features directly into Ops Hub, as well as give it the version history and archive that users previously relied on Monday Board to access. Here, PDF and form import would be centralized directly within Ops Hub, being accessible through an "Add new form" button at the top of the screen. We also decided to integrate the Search Forms feature into Ops Hub, which would allow users to view and make changes to forms after finding them, including the form's version history.

Stage 3: Low-Fidelity Sketches

Forms can be searched for and viewed directly through Ops Hub. Through the use of a dropdown from a selected form, users can view or archive it.

In a form's audit log, users can find past instances of the form, as well as what changes were made and why. To explain the changes made, users can write comments detailing the specific alteration made to the form.

Stage 4: Mid-Fidelity Designs

For our mid-fis, we made further iterations on our lo-fi sketches in Figma. Specifically, we constructed our layout around features that would best improve the overall user experience of the tool:

• Form Preview: View and download PDF forms

• Form Options: Rename, preview, and archive forms

• Audit Log: View and access past form versions

In addition to viewing or editing forms that were recently searched for, users can now upload new forms on the same screen, further optimizing efficiency.

Stage 5: High-Fidelity Designs

Our hi-fi designs used a minimalist design system to compliment the simple yet intuitive layout that we decided on during mid-fi. While we were told by SamaCare to keep the sidebar virtually identical to the one they were already using, other components were changed and iterated upon to better reflect the tool's goal of simplifying form management. We went with a clean white backdrop while adding SamaCare's signature purple color to our components as to not diverge significantly from SamaCare's existing design system, as well as to convey a message of simplicity and ease of use.

Hi-Fi Designs: View and Search Forms

Forms can be searched for using the search bar in Ops Hub, which displays all matching forms in either list view or grid view. Users can also add multiple search criteria (e.g. name and j-code) to further narrow down their search, and they can see a preview of the pdf file of the forms they find, which is now larger and can be expanded to a separate tab if needed.

Hi-Fi Designs: Edit and Update Forms

Users can edit form information including associated fax numbers and companies. It is also possible to add filters (e.g. J-Code and Benefit Type), change the form's name and description, and remove associated companies or fax numbers. Form information is directly accessible from the main view, eliminating the need to navigate to a separate tab for updates.

Hi-Fi Designs: Audit Log/Version History

The audit log allows users to see the changes that were made to a form, as well as when they were made. Users can also view multiple past versions of the same form based on the individual changes that were made to it. This way, using external tools is no longer necessary, as the audit log allows for a singular, up-to-date source of truth that is conveniently located within Ops Hub itself.

Stage 6: Final Presentation and Product Pitch

Before the day of our product pitch, we prepared an in-depth product pitch covering the research findings and design rationale of our redesign. On the day of our product pitch, we presented in front of the rest of the club, as well as a panel of industry mentors. We emphasized the tool's potential to simplify the process of PA request management by making it streamlined and reliable. Later, we presented to a board of employees from SamaCare itself, putting special focus on how our redesign will streamline daily tasks on their end.

Takeaways and Next Steps

This client project highlighted the importance of effective user research and prototyping in creating solutions that truly meet user needs, especially those of a real stakeholder. It also sharpened my skills in teamwork, communication, and pitching, setting a solid foundation for my future endeavors in tech and teaching me applicable skills in the industry.
