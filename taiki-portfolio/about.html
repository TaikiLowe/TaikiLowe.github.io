About me

I’m a product designer who speaks your language.

I’m Taiki Lowe, a multidisciplinary product designer and linguist dedicated to bridging the gap between UX and language. I’m interested in the small details that make technology feel clear, approachable, and worth returning to.

I've worked with AlygnAI and SamaCare, where I combined interface design principles and intuitive, communicative flows to streamline internal operations UX. During both experiences, I fine-tuned every button, slider, and menu to form a clear conversation with the user.

I've also contributed towards linguistic research under Dr. Laurie Lawyer at the University of California, Davis, where I utilized participant-centered UX skills to design a lexical architectures experiment free of potentially distressing stimuli.

My practice blends research, systems thinking, and visual craft. Whether I’m mapping a workflow or refining an interface, I’m focused on helping people move through products with confidence.

Outside of work, I like to go hiking, play soccer, and hang out with friends. I'm also currently studying Japanese.

Currently based in the San Francisco Bay Area and always open to thoughtful collaborations.
